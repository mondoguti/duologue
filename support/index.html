Duologue support

Duologue shows what people say as text, and translates between two languages, for talking in person. Help with the things people ask most is below.

Getting started

  • Captions (one language). Front page, Start captions. What the other person says appears as large text. Type a reply and they read it.
  • Two languages. Pick "You speak" and "They speak", then Start translating. Tap You, talk, and they read it in their language. They tap and answer; you read it in yours.
  • Watch the demo. Front page, Watch the demo. A short conversation plays by itself.

On an iPhone Duo

Open the phone. The other person reads the outer screen; you see them through the camera with their words underneath. The camera has to be on for the outer screen to light. It never takes a photo or video. If the outer screen stays dark, check that Camera is allowed for Duologue in the Settings app, and that "Give them the outer screen" is on in Duologue's settings.

It didn't hear me, or heard me wrong

  • Hold the phone closer, or pass it to whoever is talking. Distance matters more than anything else, and most of all in a loud room.
  • Tap "Say it again" to redo your last line.
  • A clip-on microphone plugged into the phone is used automatically.
  • You can always type instead: tap the keyboard button.

A word keeps coming out wrong

Settings, Words and names. Add the word the way you want it in both languages (drywall and tablaroca). There are ready-made lists for building sites, landscaping, cleaning, restaurants and front desks, and you can send your list to the rest of your crew.

Languages

English and Spanish are the checked pair. The other languages are marked Early: they work, but the fixed lines the app shows the other person have not all been checked by a native speaker. For a language to be heard on the phone itself, download it when the app offers.

Hands-free

In a conversation, turn on Hands-free and tap once. The phone keeps listening to both of you and tells from the language who spoke. It pauses itself after a few quiet minutes.

Short versions and summaries

Under a long line, "Short version" gives the gist. The list button sums up the whole conversation in both languages. Both need Apple Intelligence turned on in the Settings app, and like any summary they can miss things. The full line always stays.

Duologue Pro

Captions are free without limit. Two-language conversations are free up to a monthly number; Pro removes the limit and adds counter mode. Plans are bought and managed through your Apple Account:

  • Cancel: Settings app, your name, Subscriptions, Duologue.
  • Restore on a new phone: Duologue settings, Restore purchases.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Conversations are never recorded, there is no account, and nothing you say reaches the developer. See the privacy policy.
