Duologue privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Duologue is an iPhone app for talking with someone in person: live captions in one language, and live translation between two. This page says what the app does with what it hears and sees. The short version: Duologue has no account, no server and no analytics. We, the developer, never receive anything you say, type or show.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send us your conversations, your word list, your settings, how you use the app, or anything that identifies you or your phone. There is no sign-in. There are no advertising or tracking tools in the app.

What happens to speech

  • Listening. The microphone is open only while a conversation or captions are running and the app is on screen. Speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition.
  • On the phone when it can be. For a language whose listening model is downloaded on your phone, the speech never leaves the phone.
  • Apple's servers when it can't. For a language that is not downloaded, iOS sends the audio to Apple to turn it into text. The app tells you on screen when that is the case. Apple's handling of that audio is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.
  • No recording. Duologue never records or saves sound.

What happens to the camera

On an iPhone Duo, the camera is on during a conversation so that the outer screen can show the other person their side, and so that you can see them with subtitles. The picture is shown live and nothing else: no photo or video is ever taken, saved or sent. The other person's screen says so, in their language, for the whole conversation. On other iPhones the camera is not used for conversations.

The teleprompter, a separate part of the app, records video only when you press record, and saves it only on your phone.

What happens to the words

  • Translation is done on the phone by Apple's translation feature.
  • Short versions and summaries are written on the phone by Apple's built-in model, on phones that have it turned on. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them.
  • The written conversation is kept in memory while it is on screen and is gone when you close it, unless you turn on "Keep conversations on this phone" in Settings. Kept conversations are stored only on your phone, and the other person's screen says they are being kept. You can delete them at any time, and deleting the app deletes them all.
  • Sharing. A conversation or summary leaves the phone only if you share it yourself. The app reminds you to ask the other person first.
  • Your word list, saved phrases and settings are stored only on your phone.

Counts kept on the phone

The app keeps simple counts on your phone (for example, how many conversations you have had this month, which is how the free allowance works). They are not sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your name, your payment details or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active.

A reminder before a free trial ends is a notification made on your phone, and only if you allow notifications.

Children

Duologue is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

The other person

The person you are talking to did not install the app. So their side of the screen always says, in their language, that nothing is being recorded, and says so if the written words are being kept.

Limits you should know

Captions and translations can be wrong. Do not rely on Duologue for medical, legal or safety decisions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version is posted here.
